👨‍💼 About Me

I am an APPN Research Officer in Machine Learning and Computer Vision at The Australian National University. I work on developing intelligent scanners and processing complex plant data to generate vital plant traits for phenotyping and agricultural research.


I am also leading the Drone-to-Leaf research project, which focuses on developing an AI-enabled pipeline for moving from canopy-level drone imagery to leaf-level analytics, trait extraction, and plant health insights.


I completed my Bachelor of Advanced Computing with Honours from The Australian National University. My work combines AI, computer vision, data engineering, machine learning, and software development to build practical research tools and intelligent applications.
